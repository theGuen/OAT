All OAT numbers are octal. 1 day = 100₈ ticks (64), 1 tick = 100₈ moments (64). 1 tick = 22.5 min · 1 moment ≈ 21.1 s · 20₈ ticks (16) = 6 h. Day 0 · 00 · 00 = J2000.0 (2000-01-01 12:00, taken as UTC), so every day rolls over at 12:00 UTC. There's no local time: the same OAT value is shown everywhere on Earth. The grey arcs just divide the dial into quarters of 20₈ ticks (6 h). The orange ring shows progress through the current moment.
